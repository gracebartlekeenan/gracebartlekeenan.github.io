A writer, actor and poet, balancing on the precipice of comedy and tragedy.

A new comedy

Mercy of Sisters

Five middle-aged siblings devolve into carnage as one sister attempts to finally fly the nest.

This brand new comedy is set around Floss's 50th birthday party, where she poses a simple request to her siblings… or so she thought. After all, she has been the sole carer for their ill mother for twenty-five years, so it's fair enough she wants to get away, right? Between the rock of resentment and the hard place of intergenerational trauma, this might not be the renaissance Floss was dreaming of. Chuck in exploding secrets and illicit affairs and you might just have the family argument of the year.

You Know Those Restaurants

With the really earthy names

Where the in-house potter serves your meal on a jagged plate which looks like rubble

Nicely painted rubble.

And the in-house artist paints your portraits during the appetisers but you would really rather she didn’t as it’s only your first date

And the in-house quartet is too loud

And the in-house sommelier is making you taste the wine through his mouth

And the in-house DJ is clashing with the quartet and the in-house motivational speaker

And the in-house chef is feeding you with his fingers whilst the in-house writer offers you a poem

It goes on and on until you get carried off by the in-house undertaker and the in-house carpenter fashions you a one of a kind coffin.

All the in-housers gather to put you in the ground and the in-house gardener plants daffodils next to your head.
